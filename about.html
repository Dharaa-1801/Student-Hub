About Student Hub Portal

Designing modern, accessible, and transparent digital solutions for engineering education.


Mission & Vision

The Student Hub Portal was created to bridge the communication gap between students, faculty members, and college administration. It serves as a single digital platform where students can view their academic performance, request administrative documents, and download curriculum resources without delay.


Core System Capabilities

Feature Module Purpose & Description
Academic Management Displays subject-wise syllabus, weekly lecture schedules, lab practical slots, and faculty references.
E-Certificate Engine Enables paperless requests for Bonafide Certificates, Fee Receipts, and conduct documentation.
Accessibility Compliant Built strictly following WCAG 2.1 Web Accessibility Guidelines, keyboard navigation, and screen reader compatibility.
Instant Notification Feed Publishes circulars regarding exam schedules, GTU notices, and department events in real-time.
